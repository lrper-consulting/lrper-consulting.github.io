We support energy and industrial companies in the earliest stages of their projects, when decisions cost little and matter most. We deliver the studies that show whether a project is technically feasible, what it costs and what impact it has, before the investment is committed.

## Where we work

Capital projects advance through stages of increasing definition, known as FEL (Front-End Loading). We work in the first two.

| Stage | What is decided | Our involvement |
|---|---|---|
| FEL 1, conceptual engineering | Whether the idea is worth developing | Core service |
| FEL 2, basic engineering | Which alternative is built | As a continuation of a project started in FEL 1 |
| FEL 3 and execution | How it is built | Outside our scope |
